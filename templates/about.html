About the project

Development of a Web-Based Dashboard for Mutation Analysis and Stability Prediction of the HBB Protein in Sickle Cell Disease

This platform combines curated protein metadata, mutation interpretation, predictive stability analysis, and interactive biomedical visualization into one polished research dashboard.

Objectives

Enable quick analysis of HBB variants, structural context, and predicted stability effects in a clean and accessible interface.

Future Scope

Extend with live database-backed submissions, 3D molecular viewers, external API integration, and comparative cohort analytics.
